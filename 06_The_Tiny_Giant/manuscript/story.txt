THE TINY GIANT
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Being big is not about size. It is about the heart, and what you do with it.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), lush miniature forest world seen from a tiny scale, warm dappled sunlight through leaves, glowing bokeh, glossy toy-like rendering.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

SCALE RULE (critical for this book):
Everything in the forest is drawn at ENORMOUS scale relative to Pip. A blade of grass is taller than he is. A pebble is a hill. A mushroom is a chair or a stage. A raindrop is a bath. Keep this tiny-creature perspective on every page.

Main characters:
- Pip: a very small round creature, no taller than a daisy, with soft mint-green fur, a cream belly, huge round ears like a fennec fox, big bright amber eyes, a small black nose, tiny paws and feet, and a tuft of fluff on top of his head. Cheerful, proud, determined, and absolutely convinced he is a giant.
- Squirrel: a plump cheerful red-brown squirrel with a big fluffy tail and bright clever eyes.
- Rabbit: a small soft brown rabbit with long ears and a gentle face.
- Fox: a young orange fox cub with a white chest, big ears and hopeful eyes.
- Mice family: three tiny grey mice with pink ears.
