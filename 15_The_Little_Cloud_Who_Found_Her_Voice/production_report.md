## Story Summary
Puff is a little cloud who is afraid to speak. She longs to be loud like all the other clouds but cannot. One day Windy the gentle breeze tells her a secret: she does not need to be loud like the others. When she helps a sparrow feel heard with her gentle voice, Puff discovers a wonderful secret: your voice matters — speak up with kindness and courage. A gentle bedtime story about self-expression, kindness, and finding your own voice.
