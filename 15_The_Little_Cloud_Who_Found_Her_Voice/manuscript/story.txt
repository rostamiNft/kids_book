THE LITTLE CLOUD WHO FOUND HER VOICE
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Your voice matters — speak up with kindness and courage.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation).
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, RGB, digital reading, one image per page.

Main character:
- Puff: a cute little cloud with a soft white fluffy body, big friendly expressive eyes, rosy cheeks, small shy smile. Gentle, kind, curious, and quiet at first but finds her voice.
- Windy: a gentle breeze with a soft flowing form, kind and encouraging.
- Raindrop: a tiny sparkling water drop who is friendly and helpful.
