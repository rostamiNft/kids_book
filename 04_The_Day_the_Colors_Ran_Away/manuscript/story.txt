THE DAY THE COLORS RAN AWAY
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Notice and appreciate the beauty around you, because it is a gift.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), soft painterly storybook rendering with strong color storytelling (grayscale world vs. saturated color characters), warm volumetric light.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

COLOR RULE (critical for this book):
- In pages 2-13 the whole world is COMPLETELY GRAYSCALE - sky, trees, houses, food, clothing, everything. Only Milo's own skin and hair keep a tiny warm hint, and the crayons are gray.
- From page 14 onward, each COLOR CHARACTER is fully saturated in their own single color while the surrounding world stays gray.
- From page 18 onward, color gradually returns to the world.

Main characters:
- Milo: a cute 5-year-old boy with warm brown skin, big dark eyes, rosy cheeks, messy curly brown hair, wearing a red t-shirt and blue shorts (which turn gray in the gray world).
- Red: a small round friendly color-person entirely in bright red, with rosy cheeks, a simple red dress, big cheerful eyes, and a bouncy playful personality.
- Blue: a small round color-person entirely in bright blue, sleepy and calm, with droopy relaxed eyes.
- Green: a small round color-person entirely in fresh green, thoughtful and tidy, holding a book.
- Yellow: a small round color-person entirely in sunny yellow, cheerful and a little dramatic, always with a snack.

== PAGE 1 (FRONT COVER - PORTRAIT) ==
Title (embedded in artwork): THE DAY THE COLORS RAN AWAY
Credit (embedded in artwork): Created by Hamidreza Rostami
Art: Milo, a cute 5-year-old boy with warm brown skin, messy curly brown hair and a red t-shirt, stands in the center of a completely GRAY world, looking around in surprise with his hands slightly raised. Behind him a gray sky, gray trees and gray houses. Bursting out around him are four small glowing round color-people in bright red, blue, green and yellow, scattering away playfully with little trails of their own color. The title lettering itself is multicolored and bright against the gray.

== PAGE 2 ==
Text: Milo loved colors. He loved the red of strawberries, the blue of the sky, the green of the grass, and especially the yellow of his favorite crayon.
Art: A joyful fully saturated colorful scene: Milo sits on a sunny green lawn drawing in a sketchbook with a bright yellow crayon. Around him a bowl of red strawberries, a vivid blue sky with white clouds, lush green grass and colorful flowers. Warm sunny light, vivid and alive.

== PAGE 3 ==
Text: One morning, Milo woke up and something was... wrong.
Art: Milo's bedroom in the morning, and everything is GRAYSCALE: gray walls, gray toys, gray blankets, gray window light. Milo sits up in bed, rubbing one eye, his face puzzled and unsure. His own skin and hair keep only the faintest warm hint. Quiet, strange, slightly eerie but not scary.

== PAGE 4 ==
Text: The world was gray. Not just a little gray. Completely, totally, absolutely gray.
Art: Milo stands at his bedroom window looking out. Everything outside is gray: gray sky, gray trees, gray rooftops, gray street. Even the light feels gray. Milo's small reflection in the glass. Wide, quiet, desolate but gentle.

== PAGE 5 ==
Text: The sky was gray. The trees were gray. Even Milo's red pajamas were... gray. "That's strange," Milo said, poking his pajamas. "They were red yesterday."
Art: Close-up of Milo pulling at the collar of his gray pajama shirt, looking down at it with a confused frown, one eyebrow raised. Gray bedroom behind him. His little hands and puzzled face are the only warm thing in the frame.

== PAGE 6 ==
Text: Milo ran downstairs. "Mom! Dad! The colors are gone!"
Art: Milo bursts into the grayscale kitchen in his gray pajamas, arms out, mouth open in alarm. At the table his parents, both in grayscale clothing, look up from their gray breakfast, surprised. Gray toast, gray milk, gray plates. Warm family faces, cool gray room.

== PAGE 7 ==
Text: His parents looked up from their gray breakfast. "What do you mean, honey?" Mom asked.
Art: Milo's mother leans toward him with a kind concerned face, one hand reaching out. His father sits beside her, brows raised. Gray plates, gray cups and gray toast on the table. Milo stands at the edge of the frame holding something behind his back.

== PAGE 8 ==
Text: Milo held up his crayon box. All the crayons were gray. "The colors ran away!"
Art: Milo holds up an open crayon box toward the viewer, tilting it so we see inside: every single crayon is gray, all identical. His face is dismayed but determined. Grayscale kitchen background, soft window light.

== PAGE 9 ==
Text: Milo's dad checked the TV. It was gray. Mom looked out the window. Gray. "This is odd," Dad said.
Art: A grayscale living room. Dad kneels by an old television showing a flat gray screen. Mom stands at the large window looking out at a gray street, one hand on the curtain. Milo watches them from the middle of the room. Quiet, puzzled family moment.

== PAGE 10 ==
Text: Milo grabbed his backpack. "I'm going to find them!"
Art: Milo, now dressed in gray clothes with a gray backpack and gray sneakers, stands in the doorway with a determined face and small clenched fists. Behind him his parents exchange a worried but supportive look and small smiles. Grayscale hallway, a sliver of gray daylight outside the door.

== PAGE 11 ==
Text: Milo stepped outside. The world looked like an old movie. No red stop signs. No green grass. No blue sky. Just gray.
Art: Milo walks down his grayscale street, small against the wide scene. Gray houses, gray trees, gray sky, a gray stop sign, a gray fire hydrant. Old-film atmosphere with soft grain and gentle vignette. Lonely, curious, quiet.

== PAGE 12 ==
Text: Then he heard it. A soft giggle.
Art: Milo stops mid-step and turns his head, listening. Tiny glowing musical sparkles of colored light float at the edge of the frame, hinting at where the sound comes from. The world around him stays gray. Curious, suspenseful, delightful.

== PAGE 13 ==
Text: He followed the sound to the park. And there, sitting on a gray swing, was a small, round figure.
Art: A grayscale park with gray trees, gray grass and a gray swing set. On one swing sits a small round silhouette, the only thing in the frame with a hint of color, glowing faintly red. Milo stands at the entrance of the park, staring. Mystery and wonder.

== PAGE 14 ==
Text: It was Red. The color red, in the shape of a little person with rosy cheeks and a bright red dress. "Hello, Milo," Red said, swinging her legs. "Looking for us?"
Art: Red, a small round cheerful color-person entirely in brilliant saturated red, with rosy darker-red cheeks, a simple red dress and big bright eyes, sits on a gray swing swinging her little legs, smiling warmly. The whole park behind her stays gray. She is a vivid burst of color in a monochrome world. Milo stands in the foreground, amazed.

== PAGE 15 ==
Text: "Yes!" Milo said. "Where are all the colors? The world is so... boring without you!"
Art: Milo kneels on the gray park path, leaning in toward Red with pleading hopeful eyes and open hands. Red sits cross-legged on the gray grass, tilting her head thoughtfully with a small playful smile. Only Red has color.

== PAGE 16 ==
Text: Red giggled. "We just wanted a day off. Being a color is hard work, you know."
Art: Red laughs with her hand over her mouth, eyes crinkled, tiny sparkles of red light puffing around her. Milo sits beside her on the gray grass, smiling despite himself. A warm friendly moment in a gray park.

== PAGE 17 ==
Text: Red hopped off the swing. "Come on. I'll show you." She took Milo's hand and led him to a clearing in the park.
Art: Red holds Milo's hand and leads him along a gray winding path toward a clearing among gray trees. A warm glow of mixed colors is visible ahead through the trunks. Seen from behind. Sense of discovery, journey, anticipation.

== PAGE 18 ==
Text: There, Milo saw them all. Blue was napping under a tree. Green was reading a book. Yellow was eating a gray sandwich.
Art: A grayscale park clearing with three vivid color-people relaxing: Blue, a small round entirely-blue character, lying on the gray grass napping under a gray tree with a relaxed sleepy face; Green, a small round entirely-green character, sitting upright reading a book; Yellow, a small round entirely-yellow character, sitting cross-legged holding a sad-looking gray sandwich. Red and Milo arrive at the edge of the clearing. Beautiful contrast of color against gray.

== PAGE 19 ==
Text: "We just needed a break," said Blue, yawning. "Being everywhere all the time is exhausting." Green nodded. "And no one appreciates us. They just expect us to be there."
Art: Close group shot of the color-people. Blue stretches and yawns with a big sleepy mouth and closed eyes; Green nods seriously with her book hugged to her chest, one finger raised; Yellow beside them looks unimpressed with her gray sandwich. Red stands among them. The gray world behind. Expressive and gently funny.

Blurb (embedded in artwork):
One morning Milo wakes up and discovers that every color in the world has disappeared. Everything is gray, even his favorite red pajamas.
So Milo sets out to find the colors, and learns that they simply wanted a day off.
A warm story about noticing the beauty around us, and never taking it for granted.
