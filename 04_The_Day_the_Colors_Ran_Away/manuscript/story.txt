PAGE 5 (Story begins):
Milo loved colors.

He loved the red of strawberries, the blue of the sky, the green of the grass, and especially the yellow of his favorite crayon.

One morning, Milo woke up and something was... wrong.

[Illustration: Milo's bedroom in the morning. The walls, toys, and clothes are all in shades of gray. Milo sits up in bed, rubbing his eyes, looking confused.]

===
PAGE 6-7:
The world was gray.

Not just a little gray. Completely, totally, absolutely gray.

The sky was gray. The trees were gray. Even Milo's red pajamas were... gray.

"That's strange," Milo said, poking his pajamas. "They were red yesterday."

[Illustration: Milo looking out his window. Everything outside - the sky, trees, houses - is gray. He looks concerned.]

===
PAGE 8-9:
Milo ran downstairs. "Mom! Dad! The colors are gone!"

His parents looked up from their gray breakfast. "What do you mean, honey?" Mom asked.

Milo held up his crayon box. All the crayons were gray. "The colors ran away!"

[Illustration: Milo in the kitchen, holding up a box of gray crayons. His parents look surprised. The kitchen is all in grayscale.]

===
PAGE 10-11:
Milo's dad checked the TV. It was gray. Mom looked out the window. Gray.

"This is odd," Dad said.

Milo grabbed his backpack. "I'm going to find them!"

[Illustration: Milo putting on his backpack and shoes, determined. His parents look concerned but supportive.]

===
PAGE 12-13:
Milo stepped outside. The world looked like an old movie. No red stop signs. No green grass. No blue sky.

Just gray.

Then he heard it. A soft giggle.

[Illustration: Milo walking down his gray street. Everything is monochrome. He looks around, listening.]

===
PAGE 14-15:
He followed the sound to the park. And there, sitting on a gray swing, was a small, round figure.

It was Red. The color red, in the shape of a little person with rosy cheeks and a bright red dress.

"Hello, Milo," Red said, swinging her legs. "Looking for us?"

[Illustration: Red, a small round figure completely red (including skin, hair, dress), sitting on a swing. She looks cheerful. The park is gray except for her.]

===
PAGE 16-17:
"Yes!" Milo said. "Where are all the colors? The world is so... boring without you!"

Red giggled. "We just wanted a day off. Being a color is hard work, you know."

"But... but we need you!" Milo said.

[Illustration: Milo talking to Red. He looks pleading. Red looks thoughtful.]

===
PAGE 18-19:
Red hopped off the swing. "Come on. I'll show you."

She took Milo's hand and led him to a clearing in the park. There, Milo saw them all.

Blue was napping under a tree. Green was reading a book. Yellow was eating a (gray) sandwich.

[Illustration: A group of color figures - Blue (sleeping under a tree), Green (reading a book), Yellow (eating a sandwich), and others. They're all colored, but the world around them is gray.]

===
PAGE 20-21:
"We just needed a break," said Blue, yawning. "Being everywhere all the time is exhausting."

Green nodded. "And no one appreciates us. They just expect us to be there."

Yellow took a bite of her sandwich. "This is terrible. Food needs color!"

[Illustration: Close-up of the color figures. Blue looks sleepy, Green looks serious, Yellow looks disappointed with her gray sandwich.]

===
PAGE 22-23:
Milo thought for a moment. Then he had an idea.

"What if... what if we made a deal? You come back, and I promise to appreciate you every single day."

The colors looked at each other.

"And," Milo added, "I'll tell everyone how amazing colors are!"

[Illustration: Milo talking to the colors. He looks hopeful. The colors look at each other, considering.]

===
PAGE 24-25:
Red smiled. "That sounds nice."

Blue stretched. "Okay, but only if you promise to notice us."

Green closed her book. "And to use us wisely."

Yellow threw down her sandwich. "And to never take us for granted!"

[Illustration: The colors starting to move. Red is smiling, Blue is stretching, Green is closing her book, Yellow is tossing her sandwich aside.]

===
PAGE 26-27:
Milo held out his hands. "I promise!"

And with that, the colors began to return.

First, a touch of red on Milo's cheeks. Then, blue in the sky. Then, green in the grass.

[Illustration: Color starting to return to the world. Milo's cheeks are red, the sky has a hint of blue, the grass has a touch of green.]

===
PAGE 28-29:
The colors ran back to their places, laughing and playing.

Red painted the flowers. Blue colored the sky. Green covered the trees. Yellow brightened the sun.

The world was beautiful again.

[Illustration: The park bursting back into color. Flowers are red, sky is blue, grass is green, sun is yellow. The color figures are jumping and playing.]

===
PAGE 30-31:
Milo looked around. Everything was back to normal. Almost.

He noticed things he'd never noticed before. The way the sunlight made shadows. The different shades of green in the leaves. The little patches of blue in the sky.

[Illustration: Milo looking around his colorful world with new appreciation. He notices small details - a ladybug, different leaf colors, clouds in the sky.]

===
PAGE 32 (Final page):
From that day on, Milo never took colors for granted.

He painted pictures. He noticed sunsets. He appreciated every color he saw.

And sometimes, if he looked very carefully, he could almost see the colors waving back at him.

[Illustration: Milo painting at an easel in his backyard. The world is vibrant and colorful. In the corner, small hints of the color figures can be seen - Red peeking from a flower, Blue in a puddle, etc.]
