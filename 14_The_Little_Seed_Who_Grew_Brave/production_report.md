## Story Summary
Sprout is a tiny seed who is afraid to grow. They long to be tall like all the other plants but cannot. One day Sunny the sun tells them a secret: they do not need to grow fast like the others. When they help a ladybug feel safe with their tiny leaf, Sprout discovers a wonderful secret: courage and patience help you grow into who you are meant to be. A gentle bedtime story about bravery, kindness, and growing in your own way.
