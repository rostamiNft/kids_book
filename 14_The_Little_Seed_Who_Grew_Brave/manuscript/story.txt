THE LITTLE SEED WHO GREW BRAVE
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Courage and patience help you grow into who you are meant to be.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation).
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, RGB, digital reading, one image per page.

Main character:
- Sprout: a cute tiny seed with a soft brown shell, big friendly expressive eyes peeking out, a small shy smile, wearing a little leaf hat. Gentle, kind, curious, and shy at first but becomes brave.
- Sunny: a warm bright sun with a kind smiling face, encouraging and wise.
- Dewy: a gentle morning dew drop who is friendly and helpful.
