THE LITTLE MOON WHO WANTED TO SHINE
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: You are special just as you are — your gentle light matters to the world.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation).
Format: Landscape, 16:9 aspect ratio, RGB, digital reading.

Main character:
- Luna: a cute little moon, crescent shape with a soft silver-white glow, friendly expressive big eyes, rosy cheeks, small smile. Gentle, kind, curious, and determined.
- Stars: twinkling golden stars in the night sky.
- The Sun: warm, bright, kind and encouraging.
