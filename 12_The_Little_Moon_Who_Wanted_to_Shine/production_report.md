## Story Summary
Luna is a little moon whose light is soft and gentle. She longs to shine bright like all the stars but cannot. One night the Sun tells her a secret: she does not need to shine bright like the stars. When she helps a child sleep peacefully with her gentle silver light, Luna discovers a wonderful secret: you are special just as you are, and your gentle light matters to the world. A gentle bedtime story about self-acceptance, kindness, and shining in your own way.
