CAPTAIN WHISKERS AND THE GREAT YARN ADVENTURE
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Sometimes being part of something bigger is even better than being free.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), warm cozy homemade world with soft yarn textures and glowing afternoon light, glossy toy-like rendering.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

Setting: Grandma's cozy living room, seen from a tiny cat's-eye view. A blue yarn rug becomes the "sea". Furniture legs become forests and cliffs. Everything is warm wood, floral cushions, doilies and yarn.

Main characters:
- Captain Whiskers: a brave, plump orange tabby cat with darker orange stripes, a white chest and muzzle, big green-gold eyes, a small pink nose, white whiskers, wearing a small navy-blue captain's hat with a gold anchor badge and a red bandana knotted around his neck. Proud, curious, kind, and very sure of himself.
- The Yarn: a small tangled ball of soft teal-green yarn that wiggles and talks, with two tiny dot eyes and a little mouth formed in the strands. Sad and worried at first, then happy and smooth.
- Grandma: a warm grandmother with silver hair in a bun, glasses, a floral apron, sitting in a knitting chair. Kind and gentle.

Captain Whiskers is not an ordinary cat. He sails the high seas of Grandma's living room in a boat made entirely of yarn.

A warm story about curiosity, kindness, and discovering that being part of something bigger can be the best thing of all.
