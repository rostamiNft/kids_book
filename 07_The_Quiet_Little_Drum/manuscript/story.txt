PAGE 5 (Story begins):
In the great orchestra of Mr. Harmony's music shop, every instrument had a voice.

The trumpet blasted. The violin sang. The piano tinkled. The cymbals crashed.

And then there was Drum.

[Illustration: A busy music shop. Various instruments are playing loudly. The little drum sits quietly in the corner, looking small and overlooked.]

===
PAGE 6-7:
Drum was small and round, with a smooth wooden body and a soft leather top. He loved music. He loved rhythm. He loved the way the other instruments made the air dance.

But Drum had a secret.

He couldn't make a sound.

[Illustration: Close-up of Drum. He looks sad, his leather top smooth and untouched. Other instruments play in the background.]

===
PAGE 8-9:
"Why don't you play with us?" asked Violin, her strings humming.

Drum shook his head. "I... I can't."

"Of course you can!" said Trumpet, blasting a loud note. "Just hit your top!"

Drum tried. He tried hard. But no sound came out.

[Illustration: Violin and Trumpet talking to Drum. Trumpet is loud and enthusiastic. Violin looks curious. Drum looks embarrassed as he tries to make a sound.]

===
PAGE 10-11:
"Maybe you're broken," said Piano, her keys clicking softly.

Drum's heart sank. "Maybe I am."

He rolled into a quiet corner of the shop, where the dust gathered and the light was dim.

[Illustration: Drum rolling into a dark corner of the shop. He looks sad and defeated. Piano looks concerned.]

===
PAGE 12-13:
One day, a little girl named Lily came into the shop. She had soft brown hair and curious eyes.

She walked around, looking at all the instruments. She listened to the trumpet's blast. She admired the violin's shine. She tapped the piano's keys.

Then she saw Drum.

[Illustration: Lily walking through the music shop. She's looking at various instruments. In the corner, she spots Drum.]

===
PAGE 14-15:
"What's this?" Lily asked, picking up Drum.

"That's just Drum," said Mr. Harmony. "He doesn't work."

But Lily didn't put Drum down. She held him close and looked at him carefully.

"I think he's beautiful," she said.

[Illustration: Lily holding Drum. She looks at him with kind eyes. Mr. Harmony looks surprised.]

===
PAGE 16-17:
Lily took Drum home. She set him on her bed and talked to him like he was her best friend.

"You're going to be my special drum," she said. "Even if you can't make noise, you can still be with me."

Drum felt warm inside. No one had ever talked to him like that before.

[Illustration: Lily in her bedroom, talking to Drum. She's sitting on her bed, Drum beside her. The room is cozy with stuffed animals and books.]

===
PAGE 18-19:
One night, as Lily slept, Drum heard a sound. A soft, sad sound.

It was coming from outside. A little kitten was mewing, lost and scared.

Drum wanted to help. But how? He couldn't make noise to call for help.

[Illustration: Night scene. Lily is sleeping in her bed. Outside the window, a small kitten looks lost. Drum looks concerned.]

===
PAGE 20-21:
Then Drum had an idea.

He rolled to the window and began to tap. Not on his top—on the glass.

Tap. Tap. Tap.

The sound was soft. Very soft. But it was a sound.

[Illustration: Drum rolling to the window. He's tapping gently on the glass with his wooden edge. The kitten looks up, curious.]

===
PAGE 22-23:
Lily woke up. She heard the soft tapping and saw Drum at the window.

She looked outside and saw the kitten.

"Oh! A lost kitten!" she said.

She opened the window and brought the kitten inside.

[Illustration: Lily opening the window. She's holding the kitten. Drum looks happy.]

===
PAGE 24-25:
"Thank you, Drum," Lily said, hugging him. "You helped me find the kitten!"

Drum felt proud. He had made a difference, even without making a sound.

But then Lily had another idea.

"Let's see if we can make you work!" she said.

[Illustration: Lily hugging Drum and the kitten. She looks excited. Drum looks hopeful.]

===
PAGE 26-27:
Lily tried everything. She tapped Drum's top. She shook him. She even talked to him.

But no sound came out.

Then, Lily had a thought. "Maybe... maybe you're not meant to be loud."

She held Drum close to her ear and whispered, "Maybe you're meant to be quiet."

And then, Drum heard it.

The softest sound. Like a heartbeat. Like a secret.

It was his sound.

[Illustration: Lily holding Drum to her ear, whispering. Drum's leather top has a soft, subtle vibration. The scene is quiet and magical.]

===
PAGE 28 (Final page):
From that day on, Drum still didn't make loud noises like the other instruments.

But he made the most beautiful quiet sounds.

And Lily? She became the best listener in the world.

Because she knew that sometimes, the most important sounds are the ones you have to listen very, very carefully to hear.

[Illustration: Lily sitting with Drum and the kitten. She's listening to Drum's quiet sound. The kitten is curled up beside them. The scene is peaceful and warm.]
