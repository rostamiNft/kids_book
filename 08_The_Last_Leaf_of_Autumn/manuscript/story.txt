PAGE 5 (Story begins):
Rustle loved his branch.

It was the perfect branch. High up in the great maple tree. Just the right spot for catching the morning sun. Just the right place for feeling the cool breeze.

And it was the perfect branch for watching the world.

[Illustration: Rustle, a red maple leaf, hanging from his branch high in a tree. The morning sun shines through him. The world below looks peaceful.]

===
PAGE 6-7:
Rustle had spent all spring growing. All summer dancing in the wind.

And now, autumn had come.

The other leaves were changing. Turning gold. Turning red. Turning orange.

And one by one, they were letting go.

[Illustration: The maple tree with leaves in various autumn colors. Some leaves are falling, others have already fallen. Rustle clings tightly to his branch, still green.]

===
PAGE 8-9:
"Time to go, Rustle!" called his friend Amber, as she twirled down to the ground.

"The ground is soft!" said Golden, landing beside her. "And the wind is fun!"

But Rustle shook his stem. "I'm not ready. Not yet."

[Illustration: Amber and Golden, two leaves, floating down to the ground. They look happy. Rustle watches from his branch, looking determined.]

===
PAGE 10-11:
The days grew cooler. The winds grew stronger.

More leaves fell. The tree was getting bare.

"You'll be the last one!" called Crimson, as she fluttered down.

"That's okay," Rustle said. "I like it here."

[Illustration: More leaves falling. The tree is mostly bare. Crimson waves to Rustle as she falls. Rustle holds on tight.]

===
PAGE 12-13:
Then, one morning, Rustle noticed something.

He was changing too.

His edges were turning. From green to gold. From gold to red.

He was becoming beautiful.

[Illustration: Close-up of Rustle. His edges are turning red and gold. He looks at himself in surprise.]

===
PAGE 14-15:
"You're the most beautiful leaf on the tree!" said a blue jay, landing on a nearby branch.

Rustle blushed (if a leaf can blush). "Do you really think so?"

"Of course!" said the blue jay. "Look at you! All those colors!"

[Illustration: A blue jay on a nearby branch, talking to Rustle. Rustle is now a mix of red, gold, and orange. He looks pleased.]

===
PAGE 16-17:
But then the blue jay flew away, and Rustle was alone again.

The wind blew. Stronger this time.

Rustle held on tight. "I'm not ready," he whispered.

[Illustration: The wind blowing through the bare tree. Rustle clings tightly to his branch, his stem straining. He looks afraid.]

===
PAGE 18-19:
That night, Rustle heard a soft sound.

It was the wind, singing through the branches.

"Let go, little one," the wind whispered. "It's time."

"But I don't want to fall," Rustle said.

"Falling isn't the end," the wind said. "It's the beginning of something new."

[Illustration: Night scene. The wind swirls around Rustle. He looks thoughtful. The moon shines through the branches.]

===
PAGE 20-21:
Rustle thought about what the wind had said.

He looked down at the ground. It was covered in leaves—a soft, colorful carpet.

He looked up at the sky. It was clear and full of stars.

And he looked at himself. He was the most beautiful he had ever been.

[Illustration: Rustle looking around. Below, a carpet of fallen leaves. Above, a starry sky. He looks at his own beautiful colors.]

===
PAGE 22-23:
The next morning, the sun rose. The wind blew gently.

And Rustle made a decision.

He took a deep breath (if a leaf can breathe). He loosened his grip.

And he let go.

[Illustration: Rustle letting go of the branch. His stem is releasing. The wind catches him gently.]

===
PAGE 24-25:
The wind carried Rustle down. Not fast. Not hard. But gently, like a lullaby.

He twirled. He danced. He felt the air rush past him.

And he realized something.

Falling wasn't scary.

It was wonderful.

[Illustration: Rustle falling gently through the air. He's twirling and dancing. The sunlight catches him, making his colors glow.]

===
PAGE 26-27:
Rustle landed softly on the ground, right on top of the other leaves.

They welcomed him. "We knew you'd come!" they said.

Rustle looked around. The ground was beautiful. The leaves were beautiful. And he... he was beautiful too.

[Illustration: Rustle landing on the ground, joining the other leaves. They all look happy to see him. The ground is a colorful carpet.]

===
PAGE 28 (Final page):
That afternoon, a little girl named Emma came walking through the park.

She saw the pile of leaves and smiled. "Look at all these beautiful leaves!" she said.

She picked up Rustle and held him high. "You're the most beautiful of all!"

And Rustle knew.

He hadn't disappeared.

He had just begun a new adventure.

[Illustration: Emma holding Rustle up. She looks happy. Rustle looks proud and beautiful. The ground is covered in colorful leaves.]
