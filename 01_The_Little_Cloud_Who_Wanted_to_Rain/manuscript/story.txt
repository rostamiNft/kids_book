THE LITTLE CLOUD WHO WANTED TO RAIN
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Letting go takes courage — and it can turn you into something beautiful.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), soft pastel palette, dreamy volumetric light.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

Main characters:
- Nimbus: a small round fluffy white cloud with big friendly dark eyes, rosy cheeks, a tiny shy smile, stubby soft arms, and a soft silvery pearl-like glow. Timid, kind, and brave when it matters.
- Cumulus: a big jolly fluffy white cloud with a warm smiling face and broad soft arms.
- Stratus: a long horizontal gray cloud shaped like a cozy blanket with kind sleepy eyes.
- Sparrow: a small brown sparrow with a tiny white bandage on one wing and bright hopeful eyes.

== PAGE 20 (BACK COVER - PORTRAIT) ==
Blurb (embedded in artwork):
Nimbus is a little cloud who loves floating in the sky, but he is terrified of raining. While all the other clouds joyfully water the world below, Nimbus holds himself together tightly, afraid he will disappear.
When he sees a thirsty garden and a sad little sparrow, Nimbus discovers that letting go does not mean disappearing it means becoming part of something beautiful.
A heartwarming bedtime story about courage, kindness, and the joy of giving.
