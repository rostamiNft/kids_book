## Story Summary
Nimbus is a small cloud who loves floating in the sky, but he is terrified of raining. While all the other clouds joyfully water the world below, Nimbus holds himself together tightly, afraid he will disappear. When he sees a thirsty garden and a sad little sparrow, Nimbus discovers that letting go does not mean disappearing — it means becoming part of something beautiful. A heartwarming bedtime story about courage, kindness, and the joy of giving.
