LUNA AND THE MOONLIGHT GARDEN
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Believe in magic, and your belief can bring it back to life.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), moonlit teal and silver palette with warm accents, dreamy volumetric light, glossy toy-like rendering.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

Main characters:
- Luna: a cute 6-year-old girl with big expressive brown eyes, warm brown skin, dark curly hair in two soft buns, rosy cheeks, wearing a pale blue nightgown and soft slippers. Curious, kind, brave.
- Grandma: a warm grandmother with silver hair in a bun, gentle smiling eyes, round glasses, cozy knitted shawl. Kind storyteller.
- Ladybug: a tiny adorable ladybug with glossy red shell and soft dark spots, iridescent shimmering moonbeam wings, a small crown of tiny pink petals, big friendly eyes. Wise and playful.
