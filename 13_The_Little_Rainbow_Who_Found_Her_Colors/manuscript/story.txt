THE LITTLE RAINBOW WHO FOUND HER COLORS
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: You are special and beautiful exactly as you are — you don't need to be like everyone else.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation).
Format: Portrait orientation, RGB, digital reading, one image per page.

Main character:
- Iris: a cute little rainbow with soft pastel colors, big friendly eyes, rosy cheeks, small smile. Gentle, kind, curious, and shy at first.
- Cloud: a fluffy white cloud friend who is kind and encouraging.
- Sun: warm and bright, kind and wise.

Title (embedded in artwork): THE LITTLE RAINBOW WHO FOUND HER COLORS
