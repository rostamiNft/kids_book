## Story Summary
Iris is a little rainbow whose colors are soft and gentle. She longs to be bright like all the others but cannot. One day a fluffy cloud tells her a secret: she does not need to be bright like the others. When she helps a little girl feel calm with her gentle pastel colors, Iris discovers a wonderful secret: you are special just as you are, and your gentle light matters to the world. A gentle bedtime story about self-acceptance, kindness, and shining in your own way.
