THE ROBOT WHO WANTED TO DANCE
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: You do not have to be perfect to feel joy — just brave enough to try.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), bright friendly robot factory palette of teal, orange and cream with glowing accents, glossy toy-like rendering, soft volumetric light.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

Main characters:
- Bolt: a cute boxy silver robot with a rounded head, big glowing blue eyes on a dark face screen that shows simple happy/sad expressions, a small antenna with a glowing tip, orange accent stripes on his chest, chunky stubby arms with clamp hands and short sturdy legs. Earnest, careful, eager to please. He does NOT have a human face - his face is a screen.
- Spark: a small round cheerful maintenance robot, sunny yellow and cream, with a single big friendly eye, a little tool belt, and springy antenna arms. Bouncy and encouraging.
- Foreman Clank: a big broad friendly foreman robot, deep orange and dark gray, with a wide chest panel, thick arms, a small hard hat, and kind eyes.
