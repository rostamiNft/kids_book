PAGE 5 (Story begins):
Leo loved to paint.

He painted in the morning. He painted in the afternoon. He even painted at night, when he was supposed to be sleeping.

His room was covered in paintings—on the walls, on the floor, even on the ceiling (though his mom didn't know about that one).

[Illustration: Leo's bedroom, covered in colorful paintings. Leo is painting on a canvas on the floor. His clothes have paint splatters. The room is messy but creative.]

===
PAGE 6-7:
One night, as Leo was painting a starry sky on his ceiling, he had a strange feeling.

His paintbrush felt... different. Lighter. Almost magical.

He looked at it closely. The bristles were shimmering, like they were dusted with stardust.

[Illustration: Close-up of Leo's paintbrush. It's glowing softly with stardust. Leo looks at it with wide eyes.]

===
PAGE 8-9:
Leo dipped his brush into the paint and touched it to the ceiling.

The paint didn't just stay on the ceiling. It lifted off. It swirled. It became... real.

A tiny, twinkling star floated down and landed on Leo's palm.

[Illustration: Leo painting on the ceiling. The paint lifts off and forms a tiny, glowing star that floats down to his hand. His eyes are wide with wonder.]

===
PAGE 10-11:
Leo gasped. "Did... did I just do that?"

He painted another star. And another. Soon, his ceiling was a real starry sky, with tiny stars twinkling and dancing.

[Illustration: Leo's ceiling now has real, twinkling stars. Leo looks amazed, painting more stars.]

===
PAGE 12-13:
Leo couldn't stop. He painted a crescent moon. It hung in the air, glowing softly.

He painted a few clouds. They floated across his room like real clouds.

He painted a little house. It sat on his dresser, complete with a tiny chimney and smoke.

[Illustration: Leo's room is now magical. A crescent moon hangs in the air. Clouds float by. A tiny house sits on his dresser. Leo looks delighted.]

===
PAGE 14-15:
Then Leo had an idea. A big idea.

He painted a door on his wall. A beautiful, wooden door with a brass knob.

And when he turned the knob... the door opened.

[Illustration: Leo painting a door on his wall. The door looks real, with a brass knob. Leo reaches out to turn it.]

===
PAGE 16-17:
Beyond the door was a world of dreams.

There were talking animals. Flying fish. Trees that grew candy. Rivers that flowed with lemonade.

Leo stepped through the door and into the dream world.

[Illustration: The door is open, revealing a magical dream world. Talking animals, flying fish, candy trees, and lemonade rivers are visible. Leo looks amazed.]

===
PAGE 18-19:
"Welcome, Leo!" said a small, purple elephant with sparkly wings.

"We've been waiting for you," said a green giraffe with polka dots.

Leo looked around. "This is... this is my painting!"

The elephant nodded. "You painted us. And now we're real!"

[Illustration: Leo in the dream world, talking to a purple elephant with wings and a green giraffe with polka dots. Other magical creatures are in the background.]

===
PAGE 20-21:
Leo spent the night exploring his dream world. He rode on the back of a flying turtle. He ate candy from the trees. He drank lemonade from the rivers.

He even taught the polka-dot giraffe how to paint.

[Illustration: Leo riding a flying turtle. The polka-dot giraffe is painting on a canvas. Other magical creatures are around them, having fun.]

===
PAGE 22-23:
But as the night went on, Leo started to feel tired. His eyes grew heavy. His paintbrush felt heavy in his hand.

The purple elephant noticed. "It's time for you to go back, Leo," she said gently.

Leo nodded. "But I don't want to leave!"

[Illustration: Leo looking tired, holding his paintbrush. The purple elephant looks at him kindly. The dream world is starting to fade slightly.]

===
PAGE 24-25:
The elephant smiled. "You don't have to leave forever. You can come back anytime you paint."

Leo looked at his paintbrush. It was still shimmering with stardust.

"But... what about you?" Leo asked. "What happens when I leave?"

The elephant's wings fluttered. "We'll be here, waiting in your paintings. Ready for the next time you visit."

[Illustration: The purple elephant talking to Leo. She looks wise and kind. Leo looks at his paintbrush, then at the elephant.]

===
PAGE 26-27:
Leo gave the elephant a hug. Then he gave the giraffe a hug. Then he hugged as many of the dream creatures as he could.

Finally, he stepped back through the door into his room.

[Illustration: Leo hugging the dream creatures. Then he steps back through the door into his room. The dream world looks sad to see him go.]

===
PAGE 28-29:
The door closed behind him. The dream world disappeared.

But the stars were still twinkling on his ceiling. The moon was still glowing. The little house was still on his dresser.

And Leo knew he could return anytime.

[Illustration: Leo's room. The door is now just a painting on the wall. The stars, moon, and little house are still real. Leo looks happy and content.]

===
PAGE 30 (Final page):
The next morning, Leo's mom knocked on his door. "Leo? Are you awake?"

Leo rubbed his eyes. His room looked normal. The paintings were just paintings again.

But his paintbrush still had a little stardust on it.

And Leo knew.

The magic was real.

And it was his.

[Illustration: Leo's mom knocking on his door. Leo is in bed, looking at his paintbrush which still has a hint of stardust. He looks like he has a wonderful secret.]
