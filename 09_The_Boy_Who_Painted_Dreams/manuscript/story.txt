THE BOY WHO PAINTED DREAMS
by Hamidreza Rostami

Target audience: Children aged 3-9 years
Moral lesson: Imagination belongs to the person who makes it, and you can always go back to what you create.
Visual style: High-quality 3D animated children's illustration (cinematic children's animation), dreamy painterly rendering, glowing starlight and soft magical gradients, rich night blues against bright paint colours.
Format: Portrait orientation, 9:16 aspect ratio, 768x1376 px, RGB, one image per page, 20 pages total.

CHARACTER LOCK (repeat in every prompt):
Leo - a boy about seven years old with messy dark brown hair that sticks up at the back, big warm brown eyes, light freckles across his nose, a cheeky gap-toothed smile, wearing a paint-splattered blue T-shirt, dark shorts and one red sock and one blue sock. He carries a wooden paintbrush with a bright red handle and golden bristles that shimmer with stardust. He must look exactly the same on every page.

DREAM CREATURES (introduced later, keep designs fixed):
- The purple elephant: a small round purple elephant with big floppy ears, tiny sparkling silver wings and kind amber eyes.
- The giraffe: a green giraffe with polka dots, a long neck and a cheerful face.
- The flying turtle: a round green turtle with big patterned wings.
- Keep everything else in the dream world soft and whimsical.

SETTING: Leo's bedroom at night - sloping ceiling, a small bed under the eave, a dresser, a wooden easel, jars of paint, brushes in a tin, and walls covered edge to edge with paintings. Later: the dream world beyond the painted door.

TEXT RULE: all text is painted into the illustration. Narrative prose carries no quotation marks; spoken lines keep double quotation marks exactly. Avoid contractions that invite stray apostrophes (write "do not", "did not", "it is").

Text: Leo loved to paint. He painted in the morning. He painted in the afternoon. He even painted at night, when he was supposed to be sleeping.
Art: A bright daytime scene in a messy, joyful bedroom: Leo at a wooden easel by the window with sunlight pouring in, a brush in his hand and paint on his cheek. Canvases and finished paintings are stacked everywhere, paint jars crowd the floor, splashes of colour cover the rug. Warm, busy, full of life.

== PAGE 3 ==
Text: His room was covered in paintings. On the walls, on the floor, even on the ceiling, though his mom did not know about that one.
Art: A wide view of Leo's bedroom from the doorway, showing how every surface is covered: paintings of dragons and boats and animals on the walls, stacks on the floor, and a big starry sky painted across the sloped ceiling, which glows faintly. Leo stands small in the middle of his own colourful world, arms out proudly. Warm evening light.

== PAGE 4 ==
Text: One night, as Leo was painting a starry sky on his ceiling, he had a strange feeling. His paintbrush felt different. Lighter. Almost magical.
Art: Leo on a little step stool at night, reaching up to paint stars on the sloping ceiling, his tongue between his teeth with concentration. A warm lamp glows below, deep blue night fills the window, and the half-finished stars above him. His paintbrush is just beginning to sparkle at the tip.

== PAGE 5 ==
Text: He looked at it closely. The bristles were shimmering, like they were dusted with stardust.
Art: An extreme close-up of Leo's hand holding the paintbrush, his face behind it blurred but wide-eyed with wonder. Golden bristles sparkle with drifting stardust, tiny glowing motes lifting off the tip and swirling upward. Deep blue background, the glow lighting his fingers. Magical and hushed.

== PAGE 6 ==
Text: Leo dipped his brush into the paint and touched it to the ceiling. The paint did not just stay on the ceiling. It lifted off. It swirled. It became real.
Art: A breathtaking moment. Leo reaches up on his stool and touches his brush to the ceiling; the wet golden paint lifts away from the surface in a curling ribbon of light and spirals through the air, turning into a tiny glowing 3D star. Other painted stars trail off the ceiling around it. His face is a picture of astonishment.

== PAGE 7 ==
Text: A tiny, twinkling star floated down and landed on Leo's palm.
Art: An intimate close-up of Leo's open palm cradling a tiny real star, which glows warm gold and throws dancing light across his amazed face and up into the dark room. Delicate sparkles drift from it. He is holding his breath. Tender, luminous, magical.

== PAGE 8 ==
Text: Leo gasped. "Did I just do that?"
Art: Leo's delighted, wide-eyed face lit from below by the star in his hand, his mouth open in a gasp of wonder, his dark messy hair rimmed with light. The star's glow catches his freckles. Behind him the dark room and the first real stars hovering near the ceiling. Joyful disbelief.

== PAGE 9 ==
Text: He painted another star. And another. Soon, his ceiling was a real starry sky, with tiny stars twinkling and dancing.
Art: A wonderful wide shot: Leo on his stool painting fast, and above him a whole ceiling of REAL glowing stars, floating slightly below the painted surface, twinkling and drifting, filling the room with warm gold light. His silhouette is small and joyful beneath his own galaxy. Awe and delight.

== PAGE 10 ==
Text: Leo could not stop. He painted a crescent moon. It hung in the air, glowing softly.
Art: Leo, brush raised, having just finished a luminous crescent moon that hangs in the middle of his room like a lamp, spilling soft silver-gold light over everything. Real stars twinkle around it. Leo's face is turned up to it, glowing with pride and wonder.

== PAGE 11 ==
Text: He painted a few clouds. They floated across his room like real clouds.
Art: Soft fluffy white clouds drifting lazily through the bedroom, casting gentle shadows on the walls, one of them brushing past Leo's shoulder as he paints. He reaches up toward them with his brush, laughing. Stars and the crescent moon glow above, warm and whimsical.

== PAGE 12 ==
Text: He painted a little house. It sat on his dresser, complete with a tiny chimney and smoke.
Art: A charming close-up: a tiny real cottage, complete with a red roof, glowing yellow windows and a little chimney puffing a wisp of smoke, sitting on Leo's wooden dresser among paint jars and brushes. Leo leans in close, chin on the dresser, gazing at it with pure delight. Warm lamp light, stars and moon softly blurred behind.

== PAGE 13 ==
Text: Then Leo had an idea. A big idea.
Art: Leo standing in the middle of his starry room, brush in hand, eyes wide and bright as the idea lands. A mischievous grin spreads across his face, and behind him the painted wall, the real moon and the floating clouds all seem to lean in. He looks like he is about to do something wonderful.

== PAGE 14 ==
Text: He painted a door on his wall. A beautiful wooden door with a brass knob. And when he turned the knob, the door opened.
Art: A dramatic, beautiful moment. Leo's brush is just lifting away from a full-size wooden door that is now REAL in his bedroom wall, with carved panels, iron hinges and a round brass knob glowing warm. He reaches out with one hand and turns the knob, and a sliver of bright golden light spills out from the opening. Stars above, anticipation everywhere.

== PAGE 15 ==
Text: Beyond the door was a world of dreams. There were talking animals. Flying fish. Trees that grew candy. Rivers that flowed with lemonade.
Art: A spectacular reveal seen through the open door. Beyond the frame lies a luminous dream landscape: a candy tree heavy with striped sweets, a river of pale golden lemonade winding through soft hills, glossy fish swimming through the air with shimmering fins, and friendly animal shapes in the distance. Leo stands in the doorway, small and silhouetted, staring in wonder. Bright magical light pours into his dark bedroom.

== PAGE 16 ==
Text: Leo stepped through the door and into the dream world.
Art: Leo mid-step through the doorway, one bare foot already on the soft dream grass, looking back over his shoulder with a huge grin, his paintbrush still in his hand. The door frame glows behind him, his dark bedroom on one side and the radiant candy-coloured dream world on the other. Adventures beginning.

== PAGE 17 ==
Text: "Welcome, Leo!" said a small, purple elephant with sparkly wings. "We have been waiting for you," said a green giraffe with polka dots.
Art: A joyful meeting. The little purple elephant with big floppy ears and tiny silver wings stands in front, lifting her trunk in greeting with a warm smile; behind her the tall green polka-dot giraffe bends his long neck down to look at Leo kindly. Leo stands before them, tiny by comparison, mouth open in delight. Soft candy-coloured dream landscape all around, glowing light.

== PAGE 18 ==
Text: Leo looked around. "This is my painting!" The elephant nodded. "You painted us. And now we are real!"
Art: A warm group moment. Leo gestures around at the dream world with both arms, then points at the creatures with an amazed laugh, his paintbrush waving. The purple elephant nods encouragingly with her trunk raised, and the green polka-dot giraffe smiles beside her. Behind them a soft crowd of other painted creatures: a winged turtle, a striped creature, floating fish. Golden dream light.

== PAGE 19 ==
Text: Leo spent the night exploring his dream world. He rode on the back of a flying turtle. He ate candy from the trees. He drank lemonade from the rivers. He even taught the polka-dot giraffe how to paint.
Art: A busy, joyful montage in one scene. In the middle Leo rides high on the round green flying turtle, wings spread wide, arms up with joy. Below, the green polka-dot giraffe holds a paintbrush in his mouth, painting a wobbly sun on a big canvas with his tongue out in concentration, a candy tree behind him and the golden lemonade river winding past with floating fish above. Exhilarating, funny, full of colour.

== PAGE 20 (BACK COVER) ==
Blurb (embedded in artwork):
Leo loves to paint so much that his bedroom is covered in pictures, right up to the ceiling. Then one night his paintbrush starts to shimmer, and the stars he paints lift off the wall and become real.
A dreamy story about imagination, wonder, and discovering that the worlds you make are always waiting for you to come back.
